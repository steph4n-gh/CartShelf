# Community guidelines

CartShelf welcomes users and contributors with different levels of experience. Help each other use the app and improve the code.

- Discuss behavior, evidence, and ideas. Critique the work without attacking the person.
- Keep conversations respectful. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and unwanted personal contact are unacceptable.
- Protect privacy. Do not post another person's information, credentials, ROMs, saves, or unredacted logs.
- Keep issues and discussions relevant to CartShelf. Follow the issue templates and respect licensing boundaries.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation to keep the project usable. Use GitHub's report controls to report abusive content or accounts. [GitHub's community guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) describe the platform's reporting and moderation rules.

Report suspected security flaws through [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md).
